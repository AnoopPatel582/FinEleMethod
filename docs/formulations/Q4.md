# Q4 Element Formulation

FinEleMethod uses a four-node bilinear quadrilateral element for two-dimensional
linear elasticity. The same interpolation and strain-displacement matrix are
used for plane stress (`CPS4`) and plane strain (`CPE4`); the constitutive matrix
distinguishes the two formulations.

## Assumptions

- Small displacements and small strains.
- Linear, isotropic elasticity.
- Static loading.
- Constant element thickness (t>0).
- Two translational degrees of freedom per node: (u_x) and (u_y).
- Counter-clockwise node ordering with a positive Jacobian determinant.

The element displacement vector is

\[
\mathbf{u}_e =
\begin{bmatrix}
u_1 & v_1 & u_2 & v_2 & u_3 & v_3 & u_4 & v_4
\end{bmatrix}^{T}.
\]

## Natural coordinates and node order

The parent element occupies (-1\leq\xi\leq1) and
(-1\leq\eta\leq1).

```text
eta
 ^
 |       4 (-1,+1) -------- 3 (+1,+1)
 |          |                   |
 |          |                   |
 |       1 (-1,-1) -------- 2 (+1,-1)  --> xi
```

Its bilinear shape functions are

\[
\begin{aligned}
N_1 &= \frac{1}{4}(1-\xi)(1-\eta), &
N_2 &= \frac{1}{4}(1+\xi)(1-\eta),\\
N_3 &= \frac{1}{4}(1+\xi)(1+\eta), &
N_4 &= \frac{1}{4}(1-\xi)(1+\eta).
\end{aligned}
\]

They interpolate the physical coordinates and displacements:

\[
x=\sum_{i=1}^{4}N_i x_i,\qquad
y=\sum_{i=1}^{4}N_i y_i,
\]

\[
u=\sum_{i=1}^{4}N_i u_i,\qquad
v=\sum_{i=1}^{4}N_i v_i.
\]

## Natural derivatives and Jacobian

The natural derivative matrix is

\[
\mathbf{G}=
\begin{bmatrix}
N_{1,\xi} & N_{2,\xi} & N_{3,\xi} & N_{4,\xi}\\
N_{1,\eta} & N_{2,\eta} & N_{3,\eta} & N_{4,\eta}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

FinEleMethod forms the Jacobian as

\[
\mathbf{J}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial\xi} & \dfrac{\partial y}{\partial\xi}\\
\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial\eta} & \dfrac{\partial y}{\partial\eta}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The physical shape-function derivatives follow from

\[
\begin{bmatrix}
N_{i,x}\\N_{i,y}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\mathbf{J}^{-1}
\begin{bmatrix}
N_{i,\xi}\\N_{i,\eta}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The implementation rejects non-finite, non-positive, or numerically degenerate
Jacobian determinants. This detects invalid orientation and collapsed elements
before stiffness integration.

## Strain-displacement matrix

The engineering-strain vector is

\[
\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\varepsilon_x & \varepsilon_y & \gamma_{xy}
\end{bmatrix}^{T}
=\mathbf{B}\mathbf{u}_e.
\]

For four nodes, the (3\times8) matrix is

\[
\mathbf{B}=
\begin{bmatrix}
N_{1,x}&0&N_{2,x}&0&N_{3,x}&0&N_{4,x}&0\\
0&N_{1,y}&0&N_{2,y}&0&N_{3,y}&0&N_{4,y}\\
N_{1,y}&N_{1,x}&N_{2,y}&N_{2,x}&N_{3,y}&N_{3,x}&N_{4,y}&N_{4,x}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

## Plane-stress constitutive law

Plane stress assumes

\[
\sigma_z=\tau_{yz}=\tau_{zx}=0.
\]

For Young's modulus (E) and Poisson's ratio \(\nu\),

\[
\boldsymbol{\sigma}=\mathbf{D}_{ps}\boldsymbol{\varepsilon},
\]

\[
\mathbf{D}_{ps}=
\frac{E}{1-\nu^2}
\begin{bmatrix}
1&\nu&0\\
\nu&1&0\\
0&0&\dfrac{1-\nu}{2}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The in-plane stress vector is

\[
\boldsymbol{\sigma}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\sigma_x & \sigma_y & \tau_{xy}
\end{bmatrix}^{T}.
\]

## Plane-strain constitutive law

Plane strain assumes

\[
\varepsilon_z=\gamma_{yz}=\gamma_{zx}=0,
\]

while the out-of-plane stress \(\sigma_z\) may be nonzero. The in-plane law is

\[
\boldsymbol{\sigma}=\mathbf{D}_{pe}\boldsymbol{\varepsilon},
\]

\[
\mathbf{D}_{pe}=
\frac{E}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}
\begin{bmatrix}
1-\nu&\nu&0\\
\nu&1-\nu&0\\
0&0&\dfrac{1-2\nu}{2}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

FinEleMethod recovers the constrained out-of-plane stress after solving:

\[
\sigma_z=\frac{E\nu}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}
(\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y).
\]

## Element stiffness matrix

For either constitutive matrix \(\mathbf{D}\), the element stiffness is

\[
\mathbf{K}_e=
\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{-1}^{1}
\mathbf{B}^{T}\mathbf{D}\mathbf{B}\,t\,\det(\mathbf{J})
\,d\xi\,d\eta.
\]

FinEleMethod evaluates this integral using full (2\times2) Gauss quadrature.
The four points are

\[
(\xi,\eta)=
\left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right),
\left(+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right),
\left(+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right),
\left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right),
\]

and every one-dimensional Gauss weight is (1). Therefore,

\[
\mathbf{K}_e=
\sum_{g=1}^{4}
\mathbf{B}_g^{T}\mathbf{D}\mathbf{B}_g\,
t\,\det(\mathbf{J}_g)\,w_g.
\]

The resulting (8\times8) matrix is passed to the global COO assembly layer.
